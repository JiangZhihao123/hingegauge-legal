# HingeGauge Support

HingeGauge is a digital angle and miter gauge for supported foldable iPhones with system hinge-angle access. iOS 27.1 or later is required. Standard iPhones without a hinge cannot perform live angle measurement.

Place the two halves against the surfaces you want to measure. HingeGauge displays the angle reported by the system. Tap **Hold** to freeze a reading and **Resume** to continue. **Zero** sets a relative reference; **Clear zero** returns to the hinge angle. Equal-miter and supplementary-angle calculations always use the hinge angle, even when a relative reference is set.

**Save measurement** holds the current reading and lets you add a name. Open **Saved measurements** to review your records. Swipe a record and confirm **Delete** to remove it. Records stay on your device and do not sync between devices. No account is required.

For support, email [noahjiang2@gmail.com](mailto:noahjiang2@gmail.com) with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

## 中文帮助

HingeGauge「折角仪」适用于支持系统铰链角度读取的折叠 iPhone，需要 iOS 27.1 或更新版本。普通无铰链 iPhone 无法进行实时角度测量。

将手机两侧贴合待测表面，直接读取系统角度。点击“保持”固定读数，点击“继续”恢复测量。“归零”仅设置相对参考；斜切角和补角仍使用铰链角度计算。保存时可添加名称，在“测量记录”中查看，左滑并确认即可删除。

数据保存在本机，不需要账户，不进行跨设备同步。帮助邮箱：noahjiang2@gmail.com。

# HingeGauge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

HingeGauge is developed by Zhihao Jiang. This policy covers the HingeGauge iOS app.

## Data handled by the app

HingeGauge reads the device's system hinge angle to display measurements and calculate angles. Saved measurement names, angles, and timestamps are stored in the app's local storage on your device. The app does not transmit this information to the developer or operate an account or cloud-sync service.

The app includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

## Storage and deletion

You can delete saved measurements from the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your Apple backup settings; HingeGauge does not operate those backups.

## Purchases and support

Apple handles App Store downloads and any associated payment information under Apple's own policies. The developer does not receive your payment card details from the app.

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Support correspondence can be deleted on request, subject to applicable legal obligations.
